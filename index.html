Start teleoperation?

  
The follower arm will power on and move to the leader's pose slowly, then follow it.

  
    
    
  • Base is clamped and the motion path is clear.
    • 
    
  • Gripper is empty; the arm is in a stable supported pose.
    • 
    
  • Hold the leader still until “POSE ALIGNED”.
    • 
    
  • Sessions are limited ( s). Esc stops and holds; it is not a power cut.

Disable follower torque?

  
The arm will go limp. Support it (and any object) first.
